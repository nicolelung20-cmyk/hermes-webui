# Elevat AI Product Factory

## Purpose

A repeatable, low-cost system for discovering business workflow problems,
validating demand, producing a small digital solution, delivering it, and
measuring cash conversion. AI output is not product-market fit or revenue.

## Product lanes

1. Workflow intelligence — Revenue Leak Check, workflow maps, automation
   readiness briefs, SOP packs, and implementation plans.
2. Vertical operations kits — narrowly scoped admin workflows for care
   providers, local service businesses, and back-office teams. Do not make
   medical, legal, lending, or investment decisions.
3. Micro-software — small utilities that automate one validated workflow.
   Build only after repeated customer evidence.
4. Interactive learning products — simulations and explainers; no real
   wagering, casino cash play, brokerage execution, or financial advice.

## Factory stages and gates

| Stage | Required artifact | Exit gate |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Problem statement + named buyer | 5 buyer conversations or equivalent direct evidence |
| Lead | Qualified prospect record | Buyer, problem, urgency, and contact permission documented |
| Qualify | Workflow map + baseline | Current cost/time/error is measurable |
| Offer | Fixed-scope offer + exclusions | Buyer confirms scope and price |
| Checkout | Test-mode payment flow | Test payment and cancellation/refund paths verified |
| Delivery | Versioned artifact + handoff | Customer can use deliverable without agent intervention |
| Measure | Outcome and cash ledger | Delivery, refund, fees, and net cash reconciled |
| Improve | Change log | Change tied to observed feedback or measured outcome |
| Repeat | Reusable template | Unit economics and delivery quality remain acceptable |

## Initial catalog

- Free: Revenue Leak Check — short diagnostic identifying measurable friction;
  no guaranteed savings or earnings claims.
- $47: Workflow Brief Kit — workflow map, prioritized fixes, and 30-day plan.
- $149: Workflow Automation Audit — process inventory, risk review, and
  implementation-ready recommendations.
- $2,500: Readiness Sprint — scoped implementation engagement after discovery
  and written acceptance.

Prices are hypotheses, not validated demand or guaranteed revenue. Quote
scope, taxes, payment fees, delivery time, and refund terms before sale. No
ads, domains, SaaS, API credits, or other services without separate approval.

## Poe bot lineup (create manually in Poe; no account access assumed)

Use private bots first. Each returns structured drafts, labels assumptions,
and requests human approval before external actions.

1. **Elevat Research Analyst** — identifies narrow operational problems,
   separates evidence from assumptions, and proposes validation tests. Never
   invent interviews, market size, or citations.
2. **Elevat Offer Architect** — creates a fixed-scope offer with buyer,
   outcome, deliverables, exclusions, timeline, price hypothesis, acceptance
   criteria, and refund boundaries. No guaranteed-income claims.
3. **Elevat Product Builder** — creates the smallest useful checklist,
   spreadsheet, SOP, form, or software specification with tests and handoff.
4. **Elevat Quality & Compliance Reviewer** — checks accuracy, privacy,
   IP/license provenance, accessibility, security, tax/cash-flow context, and
   claims. Blocks unsupported or regulated claims and payment credentials.
5. **Elevat Distribution & Retention Planner** — drafts organic outreach,
   onboarding, customer success, and retention plans. No scraping, spam,
   deceptive reviews, or unapproved paid promotion.

## Required product record

Every product needs an owner/version; buyer and validated problem evidence;
IP ownership and third-party license provenance; privacy/data classification
and retention policy; claims register and review status; scope/exclusions;
price and tax/payment-fee assumptions; test-checkout evidence; delivery and
acceptance evidence; transaction/audit ledger (gross, fees, refunds, tax
reserve, net); support contact and rollback plan.

## Operating rules

- Default spend ceiling: $0. Research, drafts, local builds, and mocked tests.
- No live checkout, charge, subscription, ad spend, API-credit purchase, trade,
  bank transfer, or customer-data processing without explicit approval.
- Separate leads, qualified demand, paid orders, delivered value, and collected
  net cash. Never report a prototype or unpaid pilot as revenue.
- Start with one buyer segment and one offer; do not build speculative catalog
  items before validation.
- $55,000/week is an aspiration, not a forecast. Forecast only from observed
  conversion, average order value, delivery capacity, churn/refunds, and
  collected-cash evidence.
